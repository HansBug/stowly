# Preset data sources

All lengths are interior millimetres, weights kilograms. These are *typical* published values meant to get a plan started; real units differ by manufacturer, build year and local regulation, so check the CSC plate, the trailer data sheet or the vehicle registration before committing a load.

- **ISO containers**: external envelope from ISO 668 (20' = 6,058 mm, 40' = 12,192 mm, 45' = 13,716 mm, width 2,438 mm, heights 2,591 / 2,896 mm). Interior dimensions, door openings and payloads are the typical values on carrier specification pages (Hapag-Lloyd container specifications, OOCL container specifications); 45' payload is quoted between 25.7 and 27.6 t depending on the carrier; reefer interiors are smaller because of the cooling unit.
- **Chinese truck bodies (4.2 / 6.8 / 7.6 / 9.6 / 13 / 17.5 m)**: the customary body sizes used by Chinese carriers (货车尺寸大全 tables on cnblogs and 汽车之家, 9.6 m interior details from 天南物流). Payloads are indicative: legal limits follow GB 1589-2016 and the vehicle registration (blue-plate 4.2 m trucks are usually limited to about 1.5-2 t of cargo).
- **EU / US trailers**: typical curtainsider / dry-van interior dimensions from trailer builders' data sheets (Schmitz Cargobull, Krone; Wabash, Great Dane). The 53' interior is 52'6" x 98.5" x 110".
- **Pallets**: EPAL EUR 1 (1200 x 800 x 144), EUR 2 (1200 x 1000), EUR 6 (800 x 600); GMA / ISO 6780 48 x 40 in; ISO 6780 / JIS 1100 x 1100. The load heights (1.5 / 1.8 m) are customary stacking limits, not standards.
- **China Post cartons 1-12 and the extra-large moving carton**: the 邮政标准纸箱 series as published by carton makers (纸箱网 cnzhixiang.com, iCoA size table). Outer dimensions.
- **KLT small load carriers**: VDA 4500 (R-KLT / RL-KLT family, 600 x 400 and 400 x 300 footprints, 147 / 280 mm heights); tare weights approximate.
- **Euro stacking containers and beverage crates**: the 600 x 400 / 400 x 300 Euro footprint series (DIN EN 13199 / VDA); 20-bottle crate at about 18 kg loaded.
- **Load units**: 1000 L composite IBC on a 1200 x 1000 base (about 1.1 t loaded); US Gaylord bulk box on a 48 x 40 pallet; loaded pallets at customary heights.
- **US moving boxes**: the 1.5 / 3.0 / 4.5 cubic-foot sizes sold by moving-supply retailers.
